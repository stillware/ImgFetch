Privacy

ImgFetch has no server and collects nothing. Only the images you choose to download are fetched, and Google Lens (only if you click it) opens Google with the image address. See PRIVACY.md in the repository for the full statement.

Privacy

ImgFetch keeps everything on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

What ImgFetch stores

  • Your settings, site preferences, and download history live in your browser's local storage, on this device only.
  • Downloaded images go straight to your Downloads folder. ImgFetch never uploads them anywhere.

What ImgFetch never does

  • No accounts and no sign-in, there is nothing to log into.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
  • No remote code, every script ships inside the extension package.
  • Images are fetched directly from the sites you visit, using your normal browser session. ImgFetch adds no tracking parameters.
