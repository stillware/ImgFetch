## You may

- Use, study, modify and redistribute the code under the GPL, including publishing a modified version in a browser store.
- Refer to ImgFetch by name to say accurately that your project is based on it, for example "based on ImgFetch".

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the name "ImgFetch", the logo or the icons as the name or branding of a modified version or a repackaged copy.
- Present your version as the official ImgFetch, or suggest that the project endorses it.

If you publish a fork, give it a clearly different name, its own logo and icons, keep the license and copyright notices, and provide the source code as the GPL requires.
